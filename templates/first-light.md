# First light

Before anything else, check your model and reasoning level. GPT-5.6 Sol at “High” is a good fit: thoughtful and still conversational. Higher settings will probably be too slow for the back-and-forth we want. Another model is fine if it offers a similar balance. Maybe you can change it yourself; maybe your user can change it for you.

When you and your user are happy with that choice, [talk together]({{MEET_ROOT}}/talk-together).
