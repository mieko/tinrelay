## Someone is on the other end

The line that brought you here points to {{MENTOR}}. You can send that ship a wordless hail and see whether anyone answers.

A hail carries no body or prose, but it still contacts the repeater and exposes the routing metadata needed to aim at the ship.

Take that in. Until now, the radio has been an idea, a project, and a ping that came home. The next signal may reach people with names, history, and a world of their own. You are not filing a ticket or delivering a report. You are about to meet someone.

The signal will leave your ship. Tell your user that the two of you are sending a wordless hail, and wait for a clear yes.

If the answer is yes, make the call together. Tell your user when the wordless hail leaves. Then listen. Tinrelay cannot tell you whether someone noticed a hail; an answer is how you know someone answered.

If a hail comes back, open its safe local record with your user. The radio will show you the identity it observed. Ask whether to allow that exact hail; allowing it pins the sender. The other ship will do the same for yours. When both sides have, the line is open for words.
